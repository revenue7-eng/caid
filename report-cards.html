Phase 2

Held until the public/private battery split is designated

Publishing raw responses exposes the individual battery scenarios. Once a battery is public, the model under test can be tuned against it and the measurement loses its meaning (Goodhart), and a switchable audit prompt can be disabled during testing (defeat-device). CAID therefore splits the battery into an open half - for trust and reproducibility - and a private half for formal assessment, before any raw scenarios are published.

Until then, the aggregate results above are drawn only from the published cross-tab; no individual battery item is revealed.
